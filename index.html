Questions

Why do I have to post first?

Because the free ape is the announcement. One post per free claim — verified by the gate, enforced by the contract. Paid mints skip it entirely.

Can I claim the free one twice?

No. One per wallet, and one X account can only power one claim — both are checked on-chain.

When do I see my ape?

At reveal, when the mint window closes. Every crate opens at the same moment. Until then you hold the sealed crate — and you can trade it on the built-in market.

What happens to unminted apes?

They are never created. When the window closes, minting is closed for good.

Is there a PRMD token?

A token for holders has been announced. Nothing else — no amounts, no dates — has been promised.
